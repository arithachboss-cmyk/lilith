# Lilith Partner Outreach Queue

Last verified: 2026-08-21

This queue is for one-to-one company outreach only. It does not authorize bulk email, messaging to private communities, or transfer of renter data without consent.

## Launch Order

1. Contact the five Priority A relocation companies in `PARTNER_PIPELINE.csv`.
2. Record `last_contacted`, response and `next_action` after every touch.
3. Contact Priority B only after confirming whether the request is a referral, co-broke or inventory relationship.
4. Use one tracked link per organization. Do not replace it with the generic homepage URL.
5. Stop after one introduction and one follow-up unless the company replies or gives permission for continued contact.

## Qualification Before Handoff

- Renter has consented to being introduced to the named partner.
- Contact is reachable and the brief states THB 30,000-250,000 per month.
- A 12-month lease is confirmed.
- Area or office/school anchor, bedrooms and move date are known.
- Lead ownership, service boundary and referral/co-broke terms are agreed in writing.
- No listing is described as available until reconfirmed with the owner or listing agent.

## Follow-Up

Send once, three business days after the introduction.

```text
Hello [Team], following up on the Bangkok 12-month housing referral note below. If this sits with another colleague, could you point me to the appropriate partnerships or relocation contact? I will not send further follow-ups without a reply. Thank you.
```

## Response Labels

- `Interested`: asks for a call, terms or sample brief.
- `Correct contact`: redirects to an authorized colleague.
- `Inventory only`: can supply listings but does not refer renters.
- `Referral only`: can refer renters but does not share inventory.
- `Not a fit`: declines or operates outside the target scope.
- `No response`: no reply after the single follow-up.
