# แผนหาลูกค้าออนไลน์สำหรับ Lilith Homes

Updated: 2026-08-28

## Goal

สร้างช่องทาง lead ที่วัดผลได้สำหรับลูกค้าอสังหาไทย จีน อังกฤษ และรัสเซีย โดยเริ่มจาก MVP ที่ไม่พึ่ง paid ads อย่างเดียว:

- 30 วันแรก: ตรวจตลาด, เปิด SEO pages, เก็บ lead จริง 20-40 ราย, หา partner จีน 10-20 ราย
- 60 วัน: สร้าง repeatable pipeline จาก SEO + partner + social + relocation outreach
- 90 วัน: แยก segment ที่ปิดดีลได้จริง แล้วเพิ่มงบเฉพาะช่องทางที่มี conversion

## Market Focus

Priority segments:

- China agent referral: ลูกค้าจีนที่มี WeChat, งบชัด, ต้องการเช่าระยะยาวหรือซื้อคอนโด
- English-speaking expats: ผู้บริหาร, corporate relocation, family near international schools
- Russian-speaking clients: relocation, family, Pattaya/Phuket/Bangkok buyer or long-stay rental
- Thai premium renters/owners: บ้าน/คอนโดเช่า, เจ้าของทรัพย์ต้องการ listing ที่มี lead ต่างชาติ

Directional market note: REIC reported China, Myanmar and Russia among leading nationalities for foreign condo transfers in Q1 2025, so China/Russia language support is commercially sensible, but current campaign decisions should be validated against live lead quality and current inventory.

Source: [REIC: China, Myanmar, and Russia lead foreign condo transfers in Q1 2025](https://www.reic.or.th/News/RealEstate/470544)

## Funnel

1. Traffic source: SEO, WeChat partner, Xiaohongshu, Facebook groups, relocation partners, portal refresh
2. Landing: multilingual brief form with UTM source tracking
3. Qualification: budget, area, intent, country, language, WeChat/contact, timeline
4. Response: first reply within 15 minutes during working hours
5. Shortlist: 3-5 options with clear differences
6. Viewing: route, video viewing, availability check, owner/agent coordination
7. Close: offer, deposit, agreement, handover or transfer process
8. Partner update: status and referral record

## On-Page SEO Plan

Google Search Central emphasizes helping search engines understand content and users find the right page. The site now includes title/description, headings, internal anchors, multilingual content, Open Graph, and schema.org JSON-LD.

Sources:

- [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)
- [Google structured data intro](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data)
- [Google localized versions and hreflang](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions)

Primary page clusters to create next:

- `/rent/bangkok-condo-12-month`
- `/buy/thailand-condo-chinese-buyers`
- `/agents/china-thailand-property-referral`
- `/ru/thailand-property`
- `/owners/list-property-bangkok`

Each page should include:

- One intent-matched H1
- City/area/service keywords naturally in body copy
- Language-specific contact CTA
- Real photos or verified inventory examples
- FAQ section only where real customer questions exist
- Internal links to public lead form and partner intake
- Structured data where appropriate

## China Partner Channel

Weekly operating rhythm:

- Build a list of Chinese relocation desks, overseas property agents, student/parent networks and WeChat property communities.
- Contact only partners where outreach is allowed and relevant.
- Ask for cooperation scope before exchanging customer details.
- Use the import CSV template so partner leads carry `partnerAgency`, `partnerAgent`, `partnerContact`, `externalId`, and `importBatch`.
- Keep a short Chinese follow-up script, but record internal notes in English/Thai for team continuity.

Lead scoring signals:

- Has WeChat and contact consent
- Clear rent/purchase budget
- Specific area or city
- Timeline under 45 days
- Partner has repeatable source of customers
- Inventory can be verified before showing

## English / US-Oriented Channel

Target:

- Corporate HR and mobility teams
- Embassy/international organization staff
- International school parent networks
- Expat community groups where agent posts are permitted

Message:

- 12-month lease support
- Private shortlist instead of raw listing links
- Viewing route and contract coordination
- Bilingual documentation support

## Russian Channel

Target:

- Russian-speaking relocation partners
- Phuket/Pattaya/Bangkok family relocation groups
- Buyer leads asking for condo/villa options

Controls:

- Avoid investment-return guarantees
- Confirm payment readiness and document route before serious property work
- Route legal/tax ownership questions to qualified Thai counsel

## KPI Dashboard

Track weekly:

- Sessions by UTM source
- Form submissions
- Accepted import rows
- Rejected import rows and reasons
- Qualified lead rate
- First response time
- Shortlists sent
- Viewings booked
- Offers submitted
- Deals won/lost
- Partner source conversion

Minimum viable targets:

- 10 qualified leads/week after week 4
- 80% first response under 15 minutes during working hours
- 50%+ of accepted leads with complete country/language/contact fields
- 20%+ of qualified leads receiving shortlist within 24 hours

## 30-Day Execution Plan

Week 2:

Week 3:

- Review top source, top area, response speed and rejected rows
- Fix weak forms/scripts
- Add Russian page or partner page if early leads justify it

Week 4:

- Double down on the best 2 channels
- Prepare case study from first successful shortlist/viewing

## Risk Controls

- Do not buy scraped lead lists.
- Do not import personal data without permission from the source partner.
- Do not promise property availability until rechecked.
- Do not promise ROI, visa, tax, foreign quota or ownership outcomes without qualified review.
- Keep customer data access protected behind sign-in/token.
