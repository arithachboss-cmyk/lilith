# Retired data

These three files describe the site as the 2026-09-20 sitemap claimed it was. A direct
crawl on 2026-10-23 fetched all 82 of those URLs and found **81 return 404**; only `/`
survives. See `../../06_CRAWL_RECONCILIATION.md`.

They are kept, not deleted, because they are the record of what was believed and what
was built on it — nine clusters, two Owner decisions (D-07, D-08, both moot, neither
executed) and a cannibalisation gate that was checking against pages that do not exist.

| File | What it was |
|---|---|
| `live-inventory-sitemap-era.json` | 82 pages classified Product/Service/Knowledge/Landing |
| `clusters-sitemap-era.json` | 9 intent clusters C-1…C-9 with canonical owners and decisions |
| `c1-cluster-sitemap-era.json` | The 10 C-1 URLs with their real fetched titles |

**Do not feed these to any tool.** The live replacements are `../real-inventory.json`
and `../clusters.json`.
